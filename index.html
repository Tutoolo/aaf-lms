Week02

Model & prompt scaffolding

Pick a model. Build the prompt. Know why it's wrong before it fails on you.

What you'll come out with
  • A model-selection worksheet you'll reuse for every new agent
  • Structured prompt templates with evals attached
  • Your first single-shot agent running locally
◆ Deliverable A single-shot agent that answers one question reliably.
Week03

Tools, functions, and the first loop

Give your agent a hand. Then give it a plan.

What you'll come out with
  • Function calling wired to three real tools (yours, not toys)
  • A reasoning loop with retry, backoff, and an exit condition
  • A written post-mortem on your first loop that wouldn't stop
◆ Deliverable A multi-step agent that completes a task you actually have.
02
Act 02 · Build

Build.

Weeks 4–6. Multi-tool agents, RAG pipelines, and production deploys. You stop prototyping and start shipping.

Week04

Multi-tool orchestration

Agents that call other agents. Agents that know when not to.

What you'll come out with
  • An orchestrator pattern you can defend in a review
  • A planner-worker split, with eval on each
  • A real cost budget per task, enforced
◆ Deliverable A multi-tool agent running end-to-end on a real workflow.
Week05

Deploy to production

From localhost to a URL your mom can open.

What you'll come out with
  • A deployed agent on Vercel with a public URL
  • Postgres schema with migrations under version control
  • Observability wired — logs, errors, token spend
  • Auth gating with role-based access
◆ Deliverable Your agent live on a public URL, surviving a load test.
⚙ Launch Block Fellowship only

Pricing your agent: Three pricing frameworks for AI products, token-cost math, and a worksheet to price your own.

Week06

RAG pipelines that don't lie

Retrieval, chunking, re-ranking, evals. Making your agent cite its sources.

What you'll come out with
  • A vector store wired to your own documents
  • A chunking strategy benchmarked against three others
  • Groundedness evals catching hallucinations before shipping
◆ Deliverable A RAG-enabled agent answering from your docs, with citations.
03
Act 03 · Ship

Ship.

Weeks 7–10. Evals, security, your Demo Day pitch. You stop shipping and start selling.

Week07

Evals that catch real regressions

You can't ship what you can't test. Build the test harness.

What you'll come out with
  • Golden-set evals wired to your CI
  • LLM-as-judge evals for subjective outputs
  • A dashboard tracking quality, cost, and latency on every commit
◆ Deliverable An eval harness that blocks merges on regressions.
Week08

Security, trust, and the sales conversation

The questions enterprise buyers will ask — and the answers you'll have ready.

What you'll come out with
  • Prompt injection defenses with red-team logs
  • A data-residency and PII story for your agent
  • A written security brief you can send a procurement team
◆ Deliverable A security one-pager ready for a buyer's security team.
Week09

The pitch, the narrative, the proof

Your agent runs. Now tell someone who cares.

What you'll come out with
  • A 90-second pitch rehearsed on camera
  • A deck for a 15-minute Demo Day slot
  • A written case study with real metrics
◆ Deliverable A Demo Day pitch deck and a 90-second cold open.

Demo Day

What you'll come out with
